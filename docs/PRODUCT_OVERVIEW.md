# Product Overview

Ledora gives a business one script to install and one place to see and act on what happens on
its website: visitor analytics, chat and chatbot, lead-capture forms (embedded and hosted at
`ledora.com/{org}/{form}`), a unified lead inbox, uptime/DNS/SSL monitoring and notifications.

## Who it is for
- A business owner with one website who should not need to understand the technology.
- Marketing teams who need trustworthy numbers and lead attribution.
- Agencies running many client websites across several organizations.
- Support teams answering live conversations.

## What exists today (Phase 1)
- Accounts with email verification, sign-in, password reset and password change.
- Organizations with a public address (slug), time zone, and soft delete.
- Team management: invitations by email, five roles (Owner, Admin, Manager, Analyst, Viewer),
  role changes, removal, leaving, with an always-at-least-one-owner guarantee.
- An activity (audit) log of security-relevant changes.

## Added in Phase 2
- Websites with a one-line install snippet, live installation verification and an email when the
  first real visit arrives.
- Privacy controls per website (consent mode, Do Not Track / GPC).
- Plan usage: websites, monthly events and team seats, enforced by the server.
- Pageview and custom-event collection (dashboards arrive in Phase 3).

Everything else in the vision is on the roadmap (`DEVELOPMENT_ROADMAP.md`) and is not shown in
the product until it works.
